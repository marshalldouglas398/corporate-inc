# corporate-inc
We at Corporate INC. are proud to present our new technical interview software that is bound to help thousands of students at the University of South Carolina!

Our program offers a variety of questions that could potentially be asked in a technical interview scenario, all uploaded by companies and users who have experienced the scenarios for themselves. Users can filter through to find questions specific to their interviews, or they can scroll through our extensive list and answer whatever they like to their heart's content. Comments can be added to every question, or even to a comment itself to let explain a user's solution, ask for help, or editors can give hints on how to respond. Users can also rate questions or comments to let others know how they feel about the quality of content.

With this software, we hope to improve the student employment rate and get USC students the jobs the deserve!

## Scrum Board Feature List
- Maintain a shared bank of technical interview questions submitted by companies and users.
- Filter questions to target specific interview scenarios.
- Practice by browsing and answering questions from the full catalog.
- Add comments on questions and reply to existing comments for discussion.
- Rate questions and comments so high-quality content is easier to find.
